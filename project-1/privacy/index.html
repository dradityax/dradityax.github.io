Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Project One is a Bihar NEET / UGMAC counselling guidance platform. This page explains, in plain words, what data we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Account basics (via Google sign-in): your name, email address, and profile photo — only what Google shares with us when you sign in.
  • What you tell us: counselling-related profile details you enter yourself (for example, your category, preferences, and choices) so we can personalise guidance.

What we never collect

  • We never ask for, and never store, your NTA, BCECEB, or any exam-portal passwords.
  • We never ask you to upload identity documents, marksheets, or certificates.

How we use your data

  • To run your account and show you your dashboard.
  • To personalise counselling guidance (college predictor, reminders).
  • We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely in our database (Supabase). You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time by writing to us.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out at dradityax@gmail.com.
