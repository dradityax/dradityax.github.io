Taj Hospital and Hospitality Services is a general hospital in the heart of Araria, caring for patients and families from across the town and the surrounding areas. From everyday illness and general consultations to dental care, child health and urgent medical attention, our departments work together under one roof — with an in-house pharmacy and medical laboratory, so consultation, medicines and tests all stay within easy reach.

Patients can walk in during opening hours, and Saira Medical Centre/Store inside the hospital also serves anyone who needs medicines — patient or not.

General Hospital

Everyday medical care for the whole family — consultations, treatment and follow-up across our departments.

Pharmacy

Saira Medical Centre/Store, the in-house pharmacy inside the hospital — prescription and everyday medicines, open to patients and walk-in customers alike.

Medical Laboratory

Routine blood tests and medical investigations, carried out right here at the hospital.
