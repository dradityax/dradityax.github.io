01 — STORY

A QUIET obsession.

FACELESS, BY CHOICE

Some people collect stamps. I collect systems — the ones inside the human body. How a heart keeps time without being wound. How a nerve carries a whisper at a hundred metres a second.

I am a student of medicine, early in the walk. The coat does not fit yet, and that is fine — the learning fits. Everything here is a study, not a trophy.

The dead teach the living. I am listening.
